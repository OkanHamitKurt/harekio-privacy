Harekio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Harekio is a mobile sports and wellness application offering guided exercise programs, including fitness, yoga, Pilates, and other sports.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Harekio, including through third-party services integrated into the application.

1. Information and Workout Progress

Harekio is designed to store workout progress, completed exercises, selected programs, and preferences locally on your device.

The application is designed to work without requiring users to create a Harekio account.

2. Subscriptions and Purchases

Harekio may offer optional premium subscriptions through Google Play or Apple's App Store.

Purchases are processed by the respective app store. Harekio may use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, purchase entitlements, and related transaction information.

We do not directly receive your complete payment card details from these app stores.

3. Advertising

The free version of Harekio may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, depending on device settings and applicable consent.

Where required by applicable law, Harekio will request appropriate consent for advertising and related data processing.

4. Third-Party Services

Depending on the features enabled in the application, the following third-party services may be used:

  • Google Play Billing
  • Apple App Store In-App Purchases
  • RevenueCat
  • Google AdMob

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored workout data can generally be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the application, subject to device backup and restoration behavior.

Purchase records and certain information processed by app stores or other providers may be retained according to their applicable policies and legal obligations.

For privacy or deletion requests, please contact: harekiodestek@gmail.com .

6. Children's Privacy

Harekio is not designed specifically for children. Any age-related eligibility requirements and protections will be applied in accordance with applicable law and app store policies.

7. International Use

Harekio may be available internationally. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

8. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information processed in connection with Harekio. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: harekiodestek@gmail.com .
