Harekio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Harekio is a mobile sports and wellness application offering guided exercise programs, including fitness, yoga, Pilates and additional sports as they become available. This policy explains how information is processed when you use the app and its third-party services.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Harekio stores information such as selected sports and difficulty levels, completed exercises, workout progress, achievements and app preferences locally on your device. An account with Harekio is not required to use the core workout features.

Removing the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored progress. Device backup and restoration settings may affect whether local data is preserved. Third-party services described below may process additional information separately.

2. Steps, Activity Recognition and Health Connect

If you choose to use step tracking, Harekio may request Android activity recognition permission (android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) and Health Connect steps permission (android.permission.health.READ_STEPS). Where supported, the app reads step-count information through Health Connect or Apple Health to show daily totals, goals, recent activity and related statistics.

Step and activity information is used to provide the step-tracking feature, not to target advertising or determine advertising preferences. Harekio does not operate its own server for uploading your step history. Health and fitness permissions can be managed or revoked through your device settings or Health Connect.

If access is denied, the corresponding step-tracking features may be unavailable, while other workout features can still function.

Harekio does not sell Health Connect data or use it for advertising. Health Connect data access is limited to the features users enable. Any processing of health information remains subject to the applicable platform policies and permissions.

3. Premium Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Harekio offers optional recurring Premium subscriptions, such as monthly and yearly plans, through Google Play and, where supported, Apple's App Store. Prices, billing terms, renewals, cancellations and payments are handled by the applicable app store.

We use RevenueCat to retrieve purchase offerings, validate and manage subscription entitlements, restore eligible purchases and determine whether Premium features should be enabled.

RevenueCat may process an anonymous app-user identifier, device or app technical information, product identifiers, purchase tokens or transaction records, subscription status, renewal and expiration information, and related events needed to provide its service.

An anonymous identifier is not the same as a Harekio account registration.

We do not receive your full payment-card details through these integrations. Transaction and purchase records may be retained by the app store and RevenueCat according to their policies and applicable legal requirements.

More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy , Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy .

4. Advertising with Google AdMob

The free version of Harekio may display banner and interstitial advertisements using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Premium subscribers are not shown ads while an eligible Premium entitlement is active.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or share with Google and its advertising partners information such as IP address (which may be used to approximate location), advertising and other device or account identifiers, app interactions (including ad views and taps), and app or SDK diagnostic and performance data.

Depending on your device settings and lawful consent choices, this information may be processed to serve ads, measure their effectiveness, prevent fraud and support advertising services.

Ads may be personalised, non-personalised or limited as permitted by applicable rules and available consent signals.

Harekio does not use your Health Connect step information to personalise or target advertisements. We do not sell your locally stored workout progress to advertisers.

More information: How Google uses information for advertising and Google Privacy Policy .

5. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Harekio uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show privacy notices and request advertising-related consent or other choices before requesting ads.

For example, users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland may be asked to accept, decline or manage advertising data processing choices.

Your decision to decline advertising consent does not create a Premium subscription.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, ads may be limited, non-personalised or unavailable after your choice.

Where the app makes a privacy-options control available, you can revisit eligible advertising preferences from the app's Settings screen.

You can also manage Android advertising ID settings and relevant device privacy permissions in your device settings.

Consent preferences may be processed by Google and participating consent-management or advertising partners so your choices can be respected.

The availability of particular controls depends on your region, device and app configuration.

6. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Depending on platform and feature availability, Harekio integrates or may integrate these providers:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: advertising delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: advertising privacy notices and consent choices.
  • RevenueCat: purchase verification and Premium subscription management.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store: app-store purchases, billing and transaction administration.
  • Health Connect / Apple Health: accessing user-permitted step information for the app's activity features.

Information may be disclosed to these providers as necessary for their respective services and according to the permissions and choices applicable to you.

Their processing is also governed by their own privacy notices. Harekio does not claim that no information leaves your device: the advertising and purchase SDKs may communicate with their providers even though workout progress is stored locally.

7. Legal Bases and International Processing

Depending on your country and the type of data, processing may be based on your consent, the need to provide requested functionality or subscriptions, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests where permitted by law.

You can withdraw certain permissions or consent through the choices described above; withdrawal does not necessarily affect processing already lawfully carried out.

Some providers may process information in countries outside your place of residence. Applicable cross-border transfer safeguards and the providers' published policies govern those activities.

8. Data Retention, Deletion and Your Rights

Locally stored workout data can generally be removed by clearing Harekio's local app data or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's backup or restore behavior.

Subscription records, anonymous customer identifiers, advertising-related information and other data processed by Google, RevenueCat or app stores may be retained in accordance with those providers' policies, operational requirements and legal obligations.

Uninstalling Harekio does not automatically cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions through the store where you purchased them.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing, or to withdraw consent.

To make a privacy request, email harekiodestek@gmail.com . We may need enough information to locate relevant records and may refer you to the applicable third-party provider for data that provider controls.

9. Children's Privacy

Harekio is not directed at children and is currently intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly design the service to solicit personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app or its integrations, contact us.

10. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed in connection with Harekio. No electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if features, SDKs, data practices, service providers or legal requirements change.

The revised version and its update date will be published here.

12. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, contact harekiodestek@gmail.com .
